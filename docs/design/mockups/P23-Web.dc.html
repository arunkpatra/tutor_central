How Tutor Central treats your students’ information

A tutor keeps a record of each student to plan their classes. This page says what that record holds, what reaches the AI service and when, how a parent’s consent is asked and kept, and what a parent can ask for, in plain words.

Last changed 18 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The tutor who puts a student’s details into Tutor Central is responsible for them, as the law on personal data in India says. Tutor Central is made by GoodGround LLP, Bangalore, and keeps the records for the tutor. A parent asks the tutor about their child’s details first; anything else, write to hello@tutorcentral.in.

What is kept about a student

What the tutor enters: the student’s name, class (LKG to 10), school and board, the parent’s name and phone number, and the language the parent reads messages in.

What the tutor’s teaching adds: the chapters and skills of the student’s textbooks and how far each has come; which questions the student got right or wrong at the end of each class; who came to class; the homework given and whether it was done; marks the tutor records; the school’s tests, homework and holidays the tutor adds; and which messages the tutor sent the parent, and when.

No textbook’s text is kept, only the names of its chapters and skills.

What the AI service never needs consent for

Practice sheets, questions, worked examples, figures, a chapter brief and a mock test are written from a class level and the names of chapters and skills. Nothing about any student goes with them: not a name, not a mark, not a photo. The tutor can make this material for any student or class without asking anyone.

What goes only after the parent agrees

Some things name the student, and each goes to the AI service only after the student’s parent has agreed:

The messages the app writes to the parent (the weekly note, what a young student can now do, a word when the student is not on track, a reminder the day before a test): the student’s and the parent’s first names, the class, and the facts the message is about, such as the week’s record (what was taught, what they got right, what to practise, how many classes they came to), so it can be written in the parent’s language.

A photo of the student’s answered test, so marks can be suggested for the tutor to check, and the plan for the last days before the test made from those marks.

A photo of a report card, so its marks can be read into the record for the tutor to check.

The AI service is Claude, by Anthropic. What it reads is handled on Anthropic’s systems, which are outside India. Anthropic never uses it to train its models and normally deletes it within 30 days. Tutor Central keeps no copy of these photos, only that one was sent.

What was sent about a student for a message, a reading of marks or the plan for the last days is kept beside what came back for 90 days, so the tutor can see what a note was written from. After 90 days only what came back is kept, with a mark that something was sent and when.

How consent is asked and recorded

The app gives the tutor a message to send the parent on WhatsApp. It says what the app does with the student’s details, names the AI service, and asks for a yes. The tutor records the parent’s reply in the app: the day and the phone number it came from. The tutor can also record a yes given in person or on a call.

Until then the student’s weekly note is not written and their papers are not marked by the app; everything else works as before. The tutor sees on the student’s page that consent is waiting, and why.

What a parent can ask for

To see what is kept about their child: the tutor shows them the student’s page. To have something changed or removed: the tutor edits or deletes it in the app. To withdraw consent: the tutor removes it from the student’s page, and nothing about the child goes to the AI service from then on. To have the child removed altogether: the tutor deletes the student, and every record about them goes with it.

A parent who cannot reach their tutor can write to hello@tutorcentral.in.

Where it is kept, and who can see it

Records are kept in India, by the companies that host the app. The tutor, signed in, sees the centre’s records; one tutor can never read another’s. Tutor Central’s own staff can open a centre’s records only to help with a problem, with the reason and the time written down. They never see a parent’s number, a photo, or what was sent about a student whose parent has not agreed. Nothing is sold and nothing is shared, except with the services that run the app and the AI service as described above. The privacy page says the rest: what is kept about the tutor, how an account is removed, and that nothing watches anyone.

When this page changes

The date at the top says when this page last changed. A change that matters to a parent or a tutor will be explained here, not hidden.
