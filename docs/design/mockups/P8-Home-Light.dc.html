What it does

Students and parents
Every student with their class, fee and parent's number. Call or WhatsApp a parent from the student's page.
Attendance in a minute
Everyone starts present; tap the ones who did not come. Tell a parent on WhatsApp in one tap.
Fees by UPI
Each month's fees: who has paid, who has not. Remind a parent with your UPI id in the message, mark it paid, send a receipt.
Classes and the schedule
Your classes and the days they meet, events like a parents' meeting, and a reminder on your iPhone before each one.
Papers, homework and worksheets
Name the topic and the class; a question paper, homework or worksheet is drafted for you to check and share.
Checking and progress notes
Photograph an answer sheet with your marking scheme for suggested marks to review. Turn your observations into a note for a parent.
Your paper register
Photograph the register you already keep; the students are read off the page for you to check before they are added.

Made for how a tutor works

WhatsApp, not a new inbox
Every reminder, receipt, alert and note opens WhatsApp with the message ready. Nothing goes until you tap Send there.
Rupees and UPI
Fees in rupees. Your UPI id or your QR code goes with every reminder, so a parent can pay from the message.
Works without a connection
What you have seen stays on your iPhone. Attendance and fees you mark are sent when you are back online.
Sign in your way
Sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a code sent to your email. A password is optional.
Dark or light
The app opens dark. Light, or matching your iPhone, is one tap away in Settings.
Reads at any text size
Every screen works with the iPhone's larger text sizes and with VoiceOver.

What it does not do

No ads, no tracking
Nothing in the app or on this site watches what you do. There is nothing to sell.
Your records stay in India
Kept in a database in Mumbai. Only you, signed in, can see your centre.
Nothing is sent to parents on its own
You see every message before it goes, and you send it from your own WhatsApp.
No lock-in
Delete your account from the app and everything in your centre goes with it, at once.
