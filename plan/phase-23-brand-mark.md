# Phase 23: The brand mark

**Status:** Done (session 46, 2026-10-16): PRs #364 to #366, the website and the portal deployed, 1.0.0 (32) on TestFlight
and on the owner's iPhone. The kit moved to v1.2.0 during the phase (D122): see "As built". **Depends on:**
Phase 22. The external testing round (Phase 15's Tasks 20 to 23, D52 to D55) starts on this phase's TestFlight build.

## Goal

The app, the website and the admin portal carry the approved brand mark, `tutor_central_logo` v1.1.0 (the cap, T and C),
in place of D29's open book. The external testing round then starts on one build that carries the mark and every fix
made since build 31.

## Source

The brand kit is a sister repository at `../tutor_central_logo` (`arunkpatra/tutor_central_logo`), tag `v1.1.0`. Its
`docs/06-integration-tutor-central.md` is the runbook for this phase: which file goes where, the code for each place,
and the checks. Its rules hold here:

- The kit is the source and tutor_central holds copies. A copied asset is never edited here. A wrong asset is fixed in
  the kit and released as a new version.
- The `.icon` is generated by the kit. It is never opened in Icon Composer and saved inside tutor_central.
- Marketing never shows an iPhone Home Screen (the kit's L7). The board and the pull request may show one; the website
  and the App Store listing do not.

The runbook was written against `c00e494e` (2026-10-14). Since then Phases 21 and 22 changed neither the brand files nor
the places the runbook names (`Marks.swift`, `OpeningCover.swift`, `Info.plist`, `web/components/Brand.tsx`,
`admin/components/Frame.tsx` and `Icon.tsx`, the two `globals.css`), checked on 2026-10-16.

## Scope

1. **The board** (rule 1). One board on the design canvas, mirrored in `docs/design/mockups/`:
   - the icon in default, dark and tinted, from the kit's `previews/icon-composer/` (Apple's renders);
   - sign-in in light and dark with the new tile;
   - the launch screen;
   - the website's header and footer in light and dark;
   - the admin portal's header and its sign-in door.

   The board settles two open points: whether the charcoal tile keeps `shadowLogo`'s glow, and whether the website gets
   the link-preview image (`opengraph-image.png`).
2. **The decision and the documents** (documents only, to `main`): D120 is written, `docs/design/design-tokens.md` gets
   `iconGround` and `iconMark`, and `docs/design/components.md` gets the launch screen, the sign-in logo and the web and
   admin brand. `docs/design/information-architecture.md` gets the app icon row and the P7 launch notes, and
   `docs/design/mockups/AppIcon.svg` becomes the kit's `assets/app-icon/flat/AppIcon-default.svg`, with the kit's
   version noted beside it.
3. **The app** (one pull request):
   - the layered icon `ios/App/TutorCentral.icon` in place of `AppIcon.appiconset`, with `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME`
     set to `TutorCentral`;
   - `LaunchMark` in place of `LaunchBook` (`Info.plist`, `OpeningCover`);
   - `AppLogo` as a 56 pt `iconGround` tile with the mark at 33 pt in `iconMark`, the two tokens added the same in both
     appearances, through `Tokens+Color.swift` and its token checks (rule 3).
4. **The website and the portal** (one pull request, or one each if the pictures make one too long):
   - the mark as inline SVG from the kit's small-size master, in `Brand.tsx` (16 px in the header, 14 px in the footer)
     and in the portal's `Frame.tsx` (a `MarkIcon` in place of `BookIcon`);
   - the tab icons `icon.svg` and `favicon.ico` in both apps;
   - `apple-icon.png` on the website only;
   - the link preview if the board shows it, with `metadataBase` set;
   - `book` removed from `web/components/Icon.tsx` if nothing else uses it.
5. **The build and the round's documents.** TestFlight's next build (32), the first since build 31 (D121), carries the
   mark and #363 (formulae drawn in rows: the brief's worked example and eight more, found by the owner on build 31). A
   device test for the icon goes in `docs/testing/device-tests.md`: default, dark, clear and tinted, on light and dark
   wallpapers, in Spotlight, Settings and a notification. `docs/release.md` names build 32 for the round.
6. **Deploys.** `deploy-web` and `deploy-admin` after the merge. The API and the database are unchanged.

## Not in scope

  of rights, the trademark search and the recognition test with tutors and parents. These are the owner's, before Phase
  16's submission. The recognition test is not folded into the external testing round: it compares candidates at 2
  seconds an icon, which the round does not do.
- The profile pictures (`assets/social/`) for WhatsApp, Google and social accounts: the owner sets these himself.
- A wordmark: the name stays set in the system font (the kit's L6).
- The App Store screenshots (`docs/store/screenshots/`): they show the app's screens, not its icon.
